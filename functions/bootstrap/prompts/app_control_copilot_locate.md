Look at the phone screen the user is sharing with the co-pilot screen share, find what they should tap next for their goal, and have the app draw the highlight ring over it in the other app. Call this WHENEVER the user asks about the screen they are sharing — "can you see this / my screen", "what do I tap here", "where is the send button", "help me reply on this screen", or anything else that needs eyes on the shared screen — in voice or in chat. You do NOT see the shared screen through current_screen (that only mirrors pages inside the Ayma app); this tool is the only way to look at a co-pilot share, and calling it is what sends the current frame (frame-on-ask: nothing is sent, and frames are never stored, until you call). Pass `goal`: what the user wants to do, in their words; if they only asked "can you see this?" use their underlying task or "what you are showing me" as the goal. The return tells you exactly what happened and what to say: relay it faithfully. If it says the share is NOT on, the screen is protected (bank/password screens are black to you by design), or the vision failed, say that honestly — never claim you can see a screen this tool did not describe back to you, and never invent what is on it. If the return includes a draft for a text field, it also includes the exact readback line: say it aloud and ask before copying; only if the user says yes, call copilot_copy_draft.
