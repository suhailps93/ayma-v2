## 3. V2 additions draft (verbatim-ready copy)

### A. TERMS — new subsection under "AI-generated content and Ayma's notes and assessments"

> **Simulated conversations (including Practice Date).** Some features let
> you rehearse with a simulated persona — for example Practice Date, where
> Ayma plays a persona built from a match's visible profile so you can
> practise talking to them, with live coaching and a debrief afterwards.
> A simulated persona is **generated by AI: it is a fictional rehearsal
> aid, not the real person**, and anything it says is invented for the
> exercise — it is not a statement by, about, or endorsed by that person.
> Simulations **inform but never decide**: a practice session does not
> contact the person, does not change your matches or scores, and does not
> predict how the real person will respond. As with all AI output, practice
> content can be wrong or unflattering; use your own judgement, and never
> treat a simulation as a guarantee of a match or of any outcome.

### B. PRIVACY — append to "Why we use your data" (new bullet after the AI-persona bullet)

> - **To run practice simulations for you** — when you start a practice
>   session about a match, Ayma builds the simulated persona **only from
>   information already visible on that person's public profile** (display
>   name, age, coarse city, public stories and photo count). Private notes,
>   private photos, contact details, precise location, and anything marked
>   private are never loaded into a simulation. Your practice sessions are
>   stored privately to your account and are never shown to the person you
>   practised with.
>   _Basis: performance of our contract; legitimate interest in providing
>   an authentic AI matchmaking product._

### C. TERMS — append to "Payments and credits" (after the Refunds paragraph)

> **Subscriptions.** Paid plans sold through the Apple App Store or Google
> Play are **subscriptions that renew automatically** for each new period
> until you cancel; the price, period length, and any introductory offer
> are shown in the app and in the store before you confirm. Manage or
> cancel in your store subscription settings; cancellation stops the next
> renewal and your plan stays active until the end of the paid period.
> **Free trials:** where a plan includes a store free trial, each account
> may use **one free trial only**; a trial converts to a paid subscription
> at the end of the trial period unless you cancel first in your store
> settings.

### D. PRIVACY — append to the "Voice" row's meaning (new short paragraph at the end of "Data we collect")

> **Voice note:** voice sessions are stored as audio and text transcripts
> as described above. **Ayma does not create a voiceprint or use your voice
> to identify you** — voice is used to run the conversation and for the
> purposes in "Why we use your data", and for nothing else.
> [VERIFY: confirm no speaker-ID/voiceprint anywhere in the voice pipeline
> before this ships.]
