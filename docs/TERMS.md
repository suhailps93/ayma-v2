## Automatic analysis of your photos and public information

To build your profile and to improve the quality of the matches we suggest,
Ayma **automatically analyses**:

- **Photos you upload** — including facial analysis and physique

- **Information about you that is publicly available online** — Ayma performs a
  web search for your name and reads what it finds on the open web (for

This analysis is always and only about **you, the account holder** — your own
photos and your own name. Ayma does not run this automatic analysis on other
people, on your matches, or on anyone you are talking to.

The results are used by Ayma to understand you better and to match you more
accurately. They are kept in Ayma's own working memory and are **not shown in
your visible profile unless you ask for them** — for example by opening a
facial or physique report, or by asking Ayma to look you up on the web. When
you ask, that result is added to your profile story; until then it stays
private to Ayma.

**You cannot turn this analysis off while you use Ayma.** It is a core part of
how the service works. If you do not want your photos and public information
analysed in this way, please do not use Ayma. You can delete your account at
any time, which removes this data as described below.
