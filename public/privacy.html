Ayma Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Ayma collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. It works alongside our Terms of Service.

Ayma is an AI matchmaking app. A defining feature is that Ayma automatically analyses your own photos and public information about you to build your profile and improve your matches. That is described in the Terms and again below — please read it.

Who is responsible for your data

The company behind Ayma is Justabot LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (referred to as "Ayma", "we", or "us"). Justabot LLC is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at founder@justabot.info.

Note: If Ayma has an EU/EEA or UK presence or targets those markets, add the EU/UK representative and the Data Protection Officer (or a statement that none is required) here.

The short version

  • We collect and store what you give us (sign-in details, profile, photos, messages, voice and voice transcripts), what Ayma derives (facial and physique analyses, face data used for face recognition, a web-search summary of your name, AI notes, match scores), and device, usage and spend data. We keep it for as long as your account exists; deleting your account starts a 30-day recovery window, after which deletion is permanent.
  • We use it to run the service, suggest matches, keep users safe, meet legal duties, and improve the product.
  • We plan to show ads and to use your data for advertising, including personalised ads. No ads are shown in the current version of the app. See "Advertising" below.
  • We share it with the service providers that run Ayma's infrastructure and AI (Google Firebase including Firebase Crashlytics, Google Gemini / Vertex AI, LiveKit, RevenueCat, Sentry), with advertising partners once ads launch, with other users to the extent your profile and messages expose it, and with authorities where the law requires.
  • Ayma is not end-to-end encrypted. See "How we protect your data" below.
  • You can access, export, correct, and delete your data from within the app.

Connecting a Google account (optional)

If you choose to connect a Google account, Ayma requests your explicit consent through Google's own OAuth screen for narrow, read-only access: Gmail message metadata (subject/sender) for a bounded recent window of messages, plus the plain-text body content of a small number of those messages (after skipping senders on an exclude list that starts with major banks, payment processors, and government/tax domains, and which we can extend at any time), and Google Calendar event titles and attendee counts (never attendee identities or descriptions). Ayma reads a bounded, recent window only — never your full history — via one sync pass at connect time and periodically afterward while connected. (Google Photos was previously part of this feature, in a counts-only form; it has since been removed — Google no longer allows third-party apps this kind of access to an existing photo library. You can still share photos with Ayma directly, as described elsewhere in this policy.)

What is pulled is held in memory only, run once through Ayma's AI to distill a short set of facts about you, and then discarded; the raw Gmail/Calendar content is never written to our database. The distilled facts are added to your private working memory (the "wiki") described elsewhere in this policy, default private to you, not shown on your public profile.

You can disconnect at any time from Profile settings, which revokes Ayma's access with Google immediately (a real token-revocation call to Google, not just a local deletion) and deletes the stored access token. Facts already distilled remain in your notes unless you edit or delete them, the same as anything else Ayma has learned about you from conversation.

Why we use your data, and our legal bases

Where GDPR-style law applies, the legal bases are noted in plain terms.

  • To provide the service you asked for — create and store your profile, run chat and voice, deliver messages, keep your credit balance. Basis: performance of our contract with you.
  • To run the automatic analysis of your photos and name, keep the results in Ayma's working memory, and surface them into your profile when you ask. This is core functionality and cannot be switched off while you use Ayma. Basis: performance of our contract; and our legitimate interest in providing an AI matchmaking product. Where local law treats photo-derived analysis as sensitive data, we rely on your explicit consent, given when you create an account and add photos, and you can withdraw it by deleting your account.
  • To learn real facts about you from a connected Google account, if you choose to connect one, and distill them into your working memory. Basis: your explicit consent, given through Google's OAuth consent screen when you connect; withdrawable at any time by disconnecting, which also revokes the token with Google.
  • To suggest matches and rank compatibility. Basis: performance of our contract; legitimate interest in making the product work well.
  • For safety and integrity — review reports, investigate abuse, enforce the Terms, prevent fraud, and protect users. Basis: legitimate interest in protecting users and the service; compliance with legal obligations.
  • To improve the product — fix bugs, understand which features are used, improve model prompts and quality. We use your content for this in aggregated or limited ways and do not use it to train third-party foundation models (see "AI providers" below). Basis: legitimate interest in improving Ayma.
  • For advertising, once ads launch — to choose, personalise and measure ads (see "Advertising" below). Basis: your consent where the law requires it; otherwise our legitimate interest in funding the service through advertising.
  • To meet legal obligations — respond to lawful requests, keep records we are required to keep, handle disputes. Basis: legal obligation; establishment or defence of legal claims.
  • To send you notifications you have enabled. Basis: consent (device permission); legitimate interest for essential service messages.

Biometric information (face matching)

Ayma uses face recognition. Before you sign in you give a separate, explicit consent on the sign-in page (its own checkbox, apart from accepting these Terms and the Privacy Policy). Ayma cannot be used without it.

  • What we collect: from the photos you add (profile photos, your verification selfie, and photos you share with Ayma) we create face data, a numerical description of your face (a "face embedding"). It is not a photo and it is never shown to anyone.
  • Why, including matching against the public web: to check that a photo really shows you. We match your face data against photos found on the public web for your web search and deep dives, against the results of a reverse-image search of your own photos, against photos you share with Ayma, and against other accounts' verification selfies so one person cannot claim the free starter credits more than once. When another Ayma member runs a deep dive on you from Explore, your face data is used the same way, so the photos shown to them are actually you. Deep dives only ever look at Ayma members who have given this consent.
  • What we keep: your face data, plus the links to web photos that matched you and how closely they matched. We do not keep copies of web photos.
  • Retention: we keep your face data only while your account exists. When your account is deleted, your face data is deleted with it, on the schedule in "How long we keep your data" in the Privacy Policy.
  • Withdrawal and deletion: you can withdraw this consent at any time in Settings, under "Face matching consent". Because face matching is part of how Ayma works, withdrawing deletes your face data straight away and then deletes your account. You can also simply sign out.
  • No sale, no advertising: we never sell, lease or trade your face data, never use it for advertising, and only disclose it to the service providers that process it for us (Google, see the subprocessor list) or where the law requires.

How long we keep your data

  • We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. That includes your profile, photos, chats and direct messages, voice transcripts, AI notes ("wiki") and derived analyses, face data, match data, and your usage, credit and spend records.
  • When you delete your account (Profile, then "User settings", then "Delete account"; steps also at justabot.info/delete-account.html), we remove your profile, photos, messages, voice transcripts, AI notes and derived analyses, and your face data from our active systems.
  • Deletion has a 30-day recovery window during which you can sign back in and restore the account. After 30 days the deletion is permanent.
  • Backups are kept on a rolling basis and age out automatically, so a copy may remain in backups for a limited period after deletion.
  • We may keep specific records longer where we must for legal, tax, security, or dispute-resolution reasons (a "legal hold"), and only for as long as that purpose lasts.

How we protect your data

  • Your data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS for the app's connections to our servers, and DTLS-SRTP for voice calls over LiveKit) and encrypted at rest by our cloud providers (Google Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage default encryption).
  • Ayma is not end-to-end encrypted. Your messages, voice conversations, transcripts, photos and direct messages are not end-to-end encrypted: Justabot LLC and its service providers (cloud, AI model, voice and analytics providers) can technically access that content as needed to run, moderate, secure and improve the service, and for the other purposes in this policy.
  • Access to production data is limited to staff who need it and is authenticated.
  • Firestore and Storage security rules restrict each user's data to that user and the people they interact with.
  • No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of your data. Do not share information you are not comfortable sharing. If a breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of these rights:

  • Access and portability — get a copy of your data.
  • Correction — fix inaccurate profile information.
  • Deletion — delete your account and associated data.
  • Restriction and objection — ask us to limit or stop certain processing.
  • Opt out of advertising use — once ads launch, opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data and of targeted advertising where your local law gives you that right, and limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • Withdraw consent — where we rely on consent, withdraw it (for the core photo/name analysis and for face matching (biometric information), this means deleting your account, because both are essential to the service).
  • Non-discrimination — we will not penalise you for using these rights.

How to use them: most rights are self-service in the app — edit your profile to correct it, use the export option to get a copy, and use account deletion to erase it. For anything else, email founder@justabot.info or contact us through the in-app feedback tool. We may need to verify your identity (for example that you control the account's phone number or sign-in). We respond within the time the law allows.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. If you are in California or another US state with a privacy law, you may use an authorised agent to make a request on your behalf.
