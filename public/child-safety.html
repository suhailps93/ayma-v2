Ayma Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 2026-10-25

Ayma is a dating app operated by Justabot LLC. It is strictly for adults aged 18 and over. These standards describe how we prevent, detect, and respond to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our service.

Adults only

  • Every member must be at least 18 years old. Members confirm their age and date of birth when creating an account.
  • Accounts found to belong to anyone under 18 are closed and their data is deleted.
  • Content that sexualises minors, or that is designed to appeal to minors, is prohibited.

Zero tolerance for CSAE

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any sexual content involving a minor are strictly prohibited anywhere in the app, including profiles, photos, and messages.
  • Grooming behaviour, soliciting a minor, or arranging to meet a minor is strictly prohibited.
  • These prohibitions are part of our Terms of Service and are enforced without exception.

Reporting

  • Any user can report another user, a photo, or a conversation from within the app (report options on profiles and in chats) or through the in-app feedback tool (Profile, then "Send feedback").
  • Anyone can also report a concern by email to founder@justabot.info.
  • Reports are reviewed by our team. Accounts that violate these standards are suspended or permanently banned, and the offending content is removed.
  • Where we identify apparent CSAM or a child at risk, we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline and/or the relevant law enforcement authority, and we preserve related records as the law requires.

Compliance and contact

We comply with applicable child safety laws and respond to lawful requests from authorities. Our designated point of contact for child safety matters, who can speak to our enforcement practices, is reachable at founder@justabot.info.
