Delete your Ayma account

Last updated: 2026-10-25

Ayma is operated by Justabot LLC. You can delete your account and its data at any time. There is no charge, and you do not need to give a reason.

Delete from the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Ayma app and sign in. If you have uninstalled it, reinstall it from Google Play and sign in with the same phone number or account.
  2. Go to the You tab and open User settings.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Read the summary, type DELETE to confirm, and tap Delete my account.

You are then signed out and returned to the start screen.

If you cannot use the app

Email us at founder@justabot.info from the email address or phone number associated with your account and ask us to delete your account. We may ask you to verify that you control the account before we act, and we will confirm once deletion has started.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photos, messages, and voice transcripts.
  • The facial and physique analyses, web-search summary, and match data Ayma derived from your information.
  • Connections to linked accounts, such as Google.

The 30-day recovery window

Deletion is first held for 30 days. During this time your data is removed from the live app, and if you sign back in you can choose Recover to restore everything. After 30 days the deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What we may keep

Backups are kept on a rolling basis and age out automatically, so a copy may remain in backups for a limited period after deletion. We may also keep specific records longer where we must for legal, tax, security, or dispute-resolution reasons, and only for as long as that purpose lasts. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not by itself cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it in your store account so you are not charged again.
